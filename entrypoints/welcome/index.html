ImgurPass is installed

It checks whether Imgur is blocked for you. If it is, Imgur images load through a proxy. If it isn't, ImgurPass stays idle and sends nothing anywhere. Unofficial, not affiliated with Imgur.

ImgurPass can't reach Imgur or the proxies yet
Your browser hasn't given ImgurPass access to the few sites it needs to check for a block and load images.

What leaves your browser

  1. Your browser
    Sees an Imgur image address on a page
  2. DuckDuckGo, the ImgurPass server or a rimgo mirror
    Receives only that image address
  3. Imgur
    Sends the image to the proxy, which passes it back to you

Sent, only while Imgur is blocked

  • The address of each Imgur image, video or album you open
  • Your IP address, as with any website the proxy serves

Never sent

  • The pages you visit or anything on them
  • Analytics, crash reports or an account: there are none

DuckDuckGo and each rimgo mirror have their own privacy policies. The ImgurPass server is a backup run by the author of ImgurPass. In Settings you can turn it off, or limit ImgurPass to mirrors that say they don't collect data.

Fix Imgur images on other sites too?

Forums, blogs and chat apps embed Imgur images. To fix those, your browser needs to let ImgurPass act on every site. It will warn that ImgurPass can "read and change your data on all websites". ImgurPass only looks for Imgur image addresses and changes nothing else.

You can change this later in Settings or from the toolbar button.
